## Method

Stories were verified against at least one reputable source in the
build session on 24 Nov 2026. Numbers, names and dates were checked
against the wire. Borderline items (Poland drone, Villavicencio blast,
MNF game) are events that began late Mon local time but fall in the
Tue-UTC overnight window and were first reported Tue; they are phrased
as "overnight" / "as time expires" without claiming a specific day.
Where a wire summary was the only source, the story is attributed to
"wires" on the page. No story is invented; no speculation is presented
as fact.
